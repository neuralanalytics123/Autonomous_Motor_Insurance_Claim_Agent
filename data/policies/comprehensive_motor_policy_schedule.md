# Comprehensive Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Schedule

## COV-01-ASSIST: Travel Assistance & Roadside Recovery
Covers emergency roadside help, towing, and onward travel for Motorbikes, Vans, Passenger Cars, and Agricultural Vehicles up to EUR 1,500.00 with EUR 0.00 deductible.

## COV-02-COMPLAINT: Service & Policy Administration Redress
Covers service or policy administration complaints up to EUR 5,000.00 with a EUR 50.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer).

## COV-03-GLASS: Broken Windows & Glazing Coverage
Covers windshield, side window, and rear glass repairs/replacements up to EUR 3,000.00 with a EUR 60.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer).

## COV-04-NEGLIGENCE: Third-Party Liability & Negligence Indemnity
Covers third-party damage/liability up to EUR 150,000.00 (adjusted by vehicle type) with a EUR 250.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer).

## COV-05-ALL-RISKS: All Risks Comprehensive Physical Damage
Covers accidental vehicle damage up to the vehicle's market value (adjusted by vehicle type) with a EUR 300.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer).

## COV-06-THEFT: Vehicle Theft & Attempted Theft Coverage
Covers vehicle theft or attempted theft up to the vehicle's market value (adjusted by vehicle type) with a EUR 250.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer). Requires human specialist review.

## COV-07-INJURIES: Bodily Injury & Medical Liability
Covers bodily injury claims up to EUR 50,000.00 with EUR 0.00 deductible. Requires human review.

## COV-08-FIRE: Fire, Explosion & Lightning Coverage
Covers accidental fire damage up to the vehicle's market value (adjusted by vehicle type) with a EUR 250.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer). Requires human review.

## COV-09-OTHER: General & Ancillary Motor Perils
Covers other eligible motor claims up to the vehicle's market value (adjusted by vehicle type) with a EUR 150.00 deductible (25% discount after 5+ years with the insurer).

## EXC-01: Policy Lapse & Non-Active Coverage Exclusion
Claims on a lapsed policy with zero active policies in force are not covered.

## EXC-02: Repeated Lapse Exclusion
Claims with 3 or more policy lapses (outside roadside help and injury claims) are not covered.
